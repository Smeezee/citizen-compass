## WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE — READ THIS BEFORE DESIGNING ANYTHING

**This is measured, not assumed. It is here because this project has designed the same
feature twice on five separate occasions.**

**Ten of our 253 ships arrive with other vehicles included.** RSI states it in the bullet
list on each ship's own store page. The ten: Carrack, Carrack Expedition, Carrack w/C8X,
Carrack Expedition w/C8X, Constellation Andromeda, Constellation Aquila, Constellation
Phoenix, Constellation Phoenix Emerald, 600i Explorer, 890 Jump.

**Every vehicle they include is already a ship we carry** — C8 Pisces, C8X Pisces
Expedition, Ursa, P-52 Merlin, Lynx, P-72 Archimedes, G12, 85X. **Eight named, eight
present.** So every one of these is a link between two pages that both already exist.

**We carry no packages at all.** RSI keeps packages in a separate catalogue we do not
list. **Do not design a "packages containing this ship" section — it has no data behind
it.**

**Separately, 53 of our ships carry RSI's line "This vehicle is only available as part of
a PACKAGE."** That is availability and it changes over time. **It is not the same thing
as containment and must never share a design with it.**

**One containment claim is contested and is NOT settled:** whether the plain Carrack
includes a snub at all. Treat the ten-ship list as provisional in its membership and
solid in its shape.

## THE JOB

**Design how a ship page tells a visitor that other vehicles come with this ship, and
lets them click through to each one.**

**Answer these and nothing else:**

1. **Where on the page does it sit**, and why there rather than elsewhere.
2. **What does it say** — the exact words for the heading and for one entry.
3. **What does an entry show** beyond the name, if anything.
4. **What does the INCLUDED ship's own page say**, if anything — does a Pisces page
   mention that it comes with a Carrack, or is the relationship one-directional?
5. **What happens on a ship that includes nothing**, which is 243 of 253.

## WHAT IS NOT BEING ASKED

**Not the package section.** Not the edition or paint treatment. Not the front page.
**Not whether the Carrack Expedition card stays** — that is ruled and it is not yours.

**Do not propose data we do not hold.** No prices for included vehicles, no availability,
no images we have not got.

**Do not decide anything.** This desk rules; you recommend.

## HOW THE ANSWER IS JUDGED

**It is accepted if a builder could implement it without asking a further question**, and
if every word it puts on the page is true of data we actually hold.
