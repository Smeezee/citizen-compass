Development Progress

This does not affect the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE), which is a separate in-game/sale event and is still expected in November 2026 per the community roadmap (that's the 'Est. IAE 2026' note in the Development Progress tab). CIG has not tied the Kraken's release to CitizenCon specifically - its production timeline runs independent of the event calendar, so its lack this year doesn't change the Kraken estimate above.

General 2026 outlook (context, not confirmed ship names):
Beyond the named ships above, CIG devs said in a recent Star Citizen Live that multiple manufacturers - including Aegis, Drake, Origin, Anvil, Greycat, and Consolidated Outland - have additional ships 're-entering development' for the 2026 cycle, some of which had sat dormant as concepts for close to a decade. Not every name was made public, and some details were described by CIG as intentionally left vague. That's worth knowing about, but nothing fabricated or guessed here - no placeholder rows were added for these, since there isn't a confirmed name or manufacturer pairing solid enough to list responsibly. If a specific name surfaces later, it belongs in the table above, not as a guess now.
Known non-purchasable ships (context, not a price list):
worth knowing so a new player doesn't go looking for them in the pledge store or in-game: the RSI Bengal (a massive lore/NPC carrier, never sold), the RSI Pegasus (lore/NPC carrier), and every Vanduul alien ship (Scythe, Kingship, Void, Glaive's original alien hull before the human Esperia conversion, and a couple of other Vanduul-only craft) - these belong to the Vanduul faction in the story and are flown by NPCs/enemies only. If a new player ever sees these mentioned, they should know these are background lore ships, not something they're missing out on being able to buy.

Legend & Sources

  • Layout: manufacturer name appears ONCE as a section header. Rows underneath list only the ship name.
  • Dealer columns: a checkmark means confirmed purchasable at that location as of patch 4.9.0 (July 2026).
  • Row shading: GREEN = confirmed, currently-in-game ship - dealer location verified against a live terminal source and cross-checked for price.
  • ORANGE = not currently buyable in-game with aUEC - but this covers TWO different situations, and each note tells you which: (1) 'Concept.' ships that aren't flyable at all yet, still in development - their aUEC cell reads 'N/A - not yet flyable' - and (2) 'Flight-ready, no dealer.' ships that ARE real and flyable right now - some owned and flown by the person who compiled this sheet - they just don't have any in-game aUEC dealer selling them yet, so pledge/real-money is the only current purchase path - their aUEC cell reads 'N/A - flyable, no in-game dealer' instead, so the two are never confused. Their dealer columns show a dash either way. Their Pledge Price (USD) column IS populated where a reliable figure was found. If you're planning a real-money purchase rather than an in-game shopping trip, these are the ones to look at; if you're planning where to fly to buy something with aUEC today, skip them - they aren't purchasable that way yet.
  • Pledge Price (USD): standard/base real-money price from the RSI Pledge Store or UEX Corp's vehicle database. A dash means this wasn't sourced yet, not that the ship is unavailable for pledge.
  • A ship name with a 🔗 next to it is a clickable link straight to that ship's official RSI Pledge Store page - verified real, never guessed. Most ships don't have one yet since RSI's store URLs aren't predictable enough to generate safely; more get added as they're verified.
  • Known conflicts flagged rather than silently resolved: Aegis Idris-P shows $1,900 (current UEX figure) vs $1,500 in an older community snapshot - both noted in that ship's row, not picked for you.
  • Sources: starcitizen.tools 'Purchasing ships' (updated 2026-07-17, post-4.9.0); CStone Universal Item Finder (finder.cstone.space - community live terminal tracker, used to verify every dealer location against what's actually for sale in-game); UEX Corp vehicle database (uexcorp.space - mirrors RSI pledge store pricing); scfocus.org and CIG Monthly/Roadmap Reports (Development Progress tab); RSI Pledge Store directly (buy-links and pledge prices verified against the official store).
  • Patch context: Alpha 4.9.0 'Frontier Tensions' - LIVE build 4.9.0-LIVE.12232306, went live July 15-16, 2026. No wipe on this patch; persistence carried over from 4.8. Document version: v0.3.3 (compiled 2026-07-24).
