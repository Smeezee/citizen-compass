# JOB 1 — THE CONTENTS OF THE 14 GAME PACKAGES

**You named this as a separate job nobody had asked for. It is asked for now.**

Your own sweep is why: none of our 253 rows is a package, so the only population a
"packages containing this ship" section can be built against is RSI's own catalogue at
`/store/pledge/browse/game-packages`. **And 53 of our rows already carry RSI's sentence
"This vehicle is only available as part of a PACKAGE", which is the hook that section hangs
on.** Right now that sentence points at 14 products whose contents nobody has read.

**Same material and same method as the last two sweeps.** RSI's own store. Nothing from a
wiki, tracker or aggregator. Nothing under `/media/`. No contact with CIG.

**For each of the 14:**

    RSI's own product name
    the package's own store URL
    every vehicle it contains, by name, as RSI lists them
    for each contained vehicle: whether it is one of our 253 rows
    whether the package contains anything that is NOT a vehicle, named
      generically — say that it exists, do not itemise game currency,
      insurance terms or subscription content

**No price. We do not track them and one recorded here becomes one published later.**

**The field that decides the feature: which of our 53 package-only rows appear in which of
the 14.** A row that says "only available as part of a PACKAGE" and appears in none of the
14 is the interesting case — it means RSI has withdrawn the package that sentence refers to,
and our page would be pointing at nothing. **Say so loudly if you find one.**

**Riders that apply, both of them yours:**

- Reconcile against the known 14-item list — 14 read, 14 matched, 0 missing, 0 extra.
- **Carry one row whose answer is known from outside the sweep.** A clean reconciliation
  proves every product was read and says nothing about whether the right question was asked
  of it. Your own six-row miss is why this is now standing.

**Do not infer contents from a package's name.** "Explorer", "Industrial", "Escort" are
hints. The listing is the evidence.
