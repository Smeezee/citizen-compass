## THE SHAPE, AND IT CORRECTS MINE

Three relationships, never one list:
- **edition or paint** — same ship, cosmetically changed — folds into the ship's page.
- **true variant** — a different machine — keeps its own card and page, cross-linked.
- **package** — several ships sold together — its own section, "Packages containing this
  ship", with exact contents and a link to each included ship's own page.

## MY THREE QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

1. **Every ship in a package carries the line**, not just the biggest. The section is about
   that ship's relationships.
2. **No price, ever.** Name, contents, internal links, an official link when verified, and
   one line saying we do not track the package price.
3. **The words stay findable as searchable child terms on the canonical ship**, and the
   result must say why it matched — "Matched package: Carrack Expedition". **No duplicate
   card is kept alive merely to keep a word searchable.**

## THE CORRECTION THAT CHANGES THE BUILD ORDER

**The RSI link is not the identity of an edition.** Each edition and package gets a local
record with a verification status — verified with a date, not located, or retired — and the
link hangs off it as dated evidence. **So this ships without waiting for the 24 missing
links**, and "not located" is recorded rather than guessed.

**And do not label anything "buy" or "available".** We track neither price nor availability;
a label implying otherwise is the false-footer defect wearing a new hat.

## PAINTS

Paint records exist in `StarCitizenWiki/scunpacked-data` as `Ship.Paints` items with palette
ids. **A palette id is not a texture** and no rights-cleared browser-ready paint set was
found. **Note for whoever takes it: we already consume that dataset — the ship page cites
scunpacked snapshot 20260827T225641Z for components — so this is a rights and fidelity
question, not an access one.**

Order: names and links now; an official image only if the terms clearly cover that exact
asset; then one ship, one paint, scored against an official reference; only then the
catalogue. **Nothing hand-tinted is ever published as the real paint.**

for it.**
