## 1. DO WE SEND OUR OWN USERS TO ERKUL ON PURPOSE?

**Her recommendation:** put a clear "Open in Erkul / CSG" handoff on the loadout bench, and stop
competing on deep DPS and power optimisation. We own acquisition truth and the file-faithful
hardpoint bench; they own min-max math; we point at them deliberately.

**The case for.** We will not out-math Erkul. It is the community default for fitting and has
years of tuning in it. A tool that says "for deep optimisation, go here" is trusted more, not
less, and it is the same move as showing our own data conflicts instead of hiding them — which is
already the brand.

**The case against.** It sends traffic to the one rival closing in on our other half: Erkul v5
now does shopping, which is our core. Handing them the visitor at the moment of highest intent is
not obviously free. And there is a softer cost — a user who leaves for the math may simply stay
there next time.

**My read, and it is not a strong one.** I lean toward doing it, because the alternative is
pretending to a capability we do not have and will not build. But this is a judgement about what
the site is for, not about what is technically right, and I am not confident enough to take it
without you.

## 2. DO WE DROP THE PREVIEW GATE AND THE "TESTING" STAMP?

**Her recommendation:** drop or soften it when ready for traffic; it is friction for real users.

**What that decision actually is.** It is not a UI change. It is the decision to start being a
public product — real visitors, real first impressions, and our data wrong in public rather than
wrong in private.

**The one thing that should gate it, in my view.** Her own number-one finding is that our
verification promise is not visible on the page: `last_verified_patch` exists in the data and the
front end does not show it. **Opening the doors before that mark is on the page means the first
wave of visitors cannot tell our verified rows from our unverified ones** — which is the exact
failure the whole honesty brand exists to avoid.

**So my recommendation is a sequence rather than a yes or no:** the verification mark lands
first, the gate comes down after. If you want it down sooner than that, say so and I will order
the mark as the immediate next build instead of ranking it.
