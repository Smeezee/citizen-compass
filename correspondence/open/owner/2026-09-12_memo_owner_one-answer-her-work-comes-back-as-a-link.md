## WHY A LINK BEATS THE OBVIOUS BUILD

The obvious version is Claude in Chrome reading the conversation open in your browser.
**A link is better on four counts and none of them is convenience:**

**It does not need your browser open**, focused, or signed in — the fetch can run an
hour later.

**It is evidence.** A share URL is a re-readable record of exactly what she said, citable
in the letter that quotes it. This project's method is read the artifact, not the report
about it. **A scrape gives a report. A link gives the artifact.**

**It works from your phone**, which matters because you are often not at that desk.

**It cannot truncate silently.** A scrape of a long chat can come back partial and look
complete. A link either resolves or it does not.

## THE ONE LINE THAT MAKES IT SAFE

**Echo ends every answer with a fixed end-marker and her own section count, and the
fetch is refused without it.** Without that, a partial read becomes a design built on
half an answer with nothing to say so. **One line in her output format is the whole
difference between fragile and acceptable.**
