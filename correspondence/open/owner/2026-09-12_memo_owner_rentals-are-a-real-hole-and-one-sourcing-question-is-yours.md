## THE ONE QUESTION

**Do we ingest and republish UEX's rental price data under our own name?**

**Why it is yours and not mine.** UEX is another fan project. Their rental prices are gathered by
their community and served through their API. Taking that dataset, baking it into our pages and
showing it as Citizen Compass data is a **sourcing and publication decision**, not an engineering
one — and this project's standing rule sends anything about rights, publication or attribution to
you before anyone builds.

**What I would want in place if the answer is yes:**

- Their terms read first, by CIC, and quoted in the record rather than summarised
- Visible attribution on every row that came from them, not a credit buried in a legend
- Their freshness stamp carried through, not replaced with ours
- A named fallback for when their API changes or goes away, because it will

**What the answer being no costs us.** Rentals still ship, from CIG's own rules plus whatever we
verify ourselves — slower, thinner, and honest. **It is not a blocker, it is a speed difference.**

**My recommendation: ask them.** A short note to UEX asking whether they are willing is cheaper
than reading terms and guessing, it is the kind of thing fan projects usually say yes to, and it
turns a rights question into a relationship. **That note would be yours to send, not mine.**

## WHY RENTALS MATTER MORE THAN THE REST OF THAT SEARCH

**The site says "Know where to buy, before you fly." For a lot of hulls the honest answer to "how
do I fly this" is rent it, and we cannot say that at all.** That is a hole in the core promise
rather than a missing feature, which is why it went to the top of the design queue rather than
into the ranked list as one item among twenty.

**It also survives the no-server problem** that cut several other ideas: rental prices bake at
build time and stamp exactly like our dealer prices already do.

## WHAT I RULED WITHOUT YOU

**We do not publish a calculated rental price as a price.** The proposal's own example prints
3-day and 7-day totals next to the observed daily rate with nothing marking them as multiplied —
I re-derived every one of them and they are all computed. **Where we have not seen the number, we
show CIG's rule ("3 days: 10% off") instead of a figure.** Same reasoning as refusing fake
component prices and guessed livery colours.

**The break-even idea is the best thing in either document** — roughly 36 days of Vulture rental
equals its buy price; I checked the arithmetic. **It ships with the caveat attached**, because a
rental is stock-only and ends, and a bare day count implies the two purchases are the same.
