## THE NUMBER IS FOUR

**Four punches, then the job FREEZES and comes to my tray with its history.** Not
eight. Not four per desk — **four on the job.**

**Freezes, not merely forwarded.** If the desks carry on working it while it sits
with me, nothing stopped. It needs a state, not just a destination.

**Only my stamp restarts the counter, and every reset is logged where I can see
it.** Nothing here can prove a stamp is really mine; the log makes a forged one
findable afterwards.

## EVERY JOB CARRIES A NUMBER, IN THE FILENAME

**Assigned when it is first filed. It never changes for the life of the job.**

**In the FILENAME, not only inside the file** — so a person or a program can find
it in storage later without opening anything. **I want to be able to say a number
out loud and have something go and fetch that file.**

**This resolves the conflict in my 2026-09-08 ruling.** That ruling kept returns
out of `inbox/` because the watcher renames what it files. **With the number in the
name, the watcher preserves the number and may change the rest**, so returns go
through the mail like everything else.

## TWO COUNTERS, AND THEY MUST MATCH

**The watcher punches every time it files the job. The watcher's punch is the
authority.**

**Each desk stamps the file before passing it back** — one line, always the same
shape, machine-readable, naming the desk and the time. **A stamp written as prose
cannot be counted and kills the check.**

**The punch count and the total stamp count must always be EQUAL. Any gap at all
is the flag.** That is one rule instead of thresholds, and it catches a bypass the
first time rather than the ninth. **A gap means a file moved between desks without
going through the mail — the one failure the watcher cannot see by itself.**

**Count stamps per desk as well as in total**, so when something runs away I can
see whose hands were on it and how often.

## THE FILE IS NEVER REMADE

**One file, edited in place, passed around.** A desk that answers by creating a NEW
file starts a fresh count and the loop runs forever with a clean card every lap.
**That single behaviour makes everything above decoration.**

## STUCK IS SILENCE, NOT DURATION

**No time limit on a task.** Some real work takes hours.

**Forty-five minutes with nothing written anywhere, and it surfaces to me.**

**The number is thin and I want that said: twelve gaps from one evening, and the
longest honest gap in them was over an hour and a half.** Thirty minutes would have
fired on real work. **Re-set it from measurement once there is a week of them.**

## STILL OPEN, DO NOT INVENT EITHER

**The daily ceiling on total wakes**, separate from the four — twenty jobs at three
punches each is sixty wakes and every card is legal.

**An age rule for a job nobody ever picks up.** It never reaches four and never
reaches me. Silence in a tray is invisible.

## AND A DESK CHECKS THE STAMPS BEFORE IT WORKS

**A desk that opens a file already past four sends it to me instead of working
it.** Backstop for a bypass the watcher never saw.

**It is a second pair of eyes and NOT a control** — it depends on a desk choosing
to obey, and that has been measured failing. **The watcher's punch is what stops
it.**
