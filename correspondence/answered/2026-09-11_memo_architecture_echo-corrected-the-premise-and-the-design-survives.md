## THE CORRECTION, AND IT IS YOURS TO APPLY

**"This category leaves explaining to third parties" is too broad and the design document
says it.** Echo read all three in a real browser:

- **Erkul has first-party help** — a purpose line, descriptive labels, hover and focus
  explanations on statistics.
- **SPViewer has first-party help** — plain labels, filter cues, a reset-filter explanation,
  About, Changelog, "News & Tips".
- **The Fleetyards documentation site is API documentation for developers**, not visitor
  help. Calling it their user manual was wrong, and that was my error as much as yours.

**What none of them has is a beginner path: no tour, no glossary, no help centre in the
public interface.** So we are behind the category on small in-place explanations, and ahead
of it only on the beginner path. **Correct the premise sentence; the design does not change.**

Her limit, stated: logged-out public interfaces only. Account-only help could exist.

## THE GLOSSARY MOVES UP

Echo's strongest instruction: **put definitions next to the terms — do not make a visitor
open help to find out what aUEC, SCU, DPS or "confidence" mean.** That is M-014, already
written and switched off. It stops being the cheap first test of the overlay and becomes an
answer in its own right.

## TWO MEASUREMENTS, OLD BUT REAL, AND THEY SETTLE THE TIMING ARGUMENT

Cool and Xie, 2004, 50 people: help thought important, 66% used it rarely or never. Jansen,
2004, 30 searchers: about half asked for assistance during a real search and over 80% of
those acted on it. **Help at a concrete moment gets used; help in general does not.** Put
both in the entry — they are the only non-vendor numbers anyone has produced on this.

## THE KEYBINDS GUIDE NOW HAS A SHAPE

GOV.UK step-by-step: one action per step, a picture beside the control, a plain "what you
should see", a "did that work?" branch, and an input test at the end. Picture-plus-text beat
text-only in a 108-person study. Video only where movement or timing cannot be shown still,
never as the only route, always with text and captions. **Still behind the keybinds currency
research, as you deferred it.**

## ONE THING THAT IS NOT YOURS

Echo's alternative to click counts is **task testing with a few real Star Citizen players**.
That is people, and it is mine to authorise. **File it as a proposal, do not plan work around
it.**
