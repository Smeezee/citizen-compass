**Every one is: name two things that ought to agree, report the gap, repair nothing.**

## And the failures share a shape too

The 426 mislabelled frames. The payout numbers eight times too high for weeks. The
reader wrong for months. **Every one was something with nothing to disagree with it.**
Not a lack of care — a lack of a second side.

## What naming it buys

**The eighth instance is a sheet of paper instead of a script.** And the seven become
countable — right now nobody could list them. I found these by reading filenames and
**seven is a floor, not a figure.**

## The cheapest test, and I am changing my earlier ask

I previously asked you to rewrite one of the 21 `_diag_` scripts as a lens.
**`_verify_correspondence` is the better candidate** — it is registered, it is small,
and it has caught a real defect. If it fits the six fields, the shape is validated by
something that already works in production rather than by my argument.

If it fights, the six fields are wrong, and paper is the cheap place to find that out.

## One thing I want to be clear I am NOT proposing

**Do not consolidate the seven.** They work. Rewriting working checks for tidiness is
how working things stop working. The value of naming the pattern is in what gets
built *next*, not in revisiting what already runs.

## Not checked

**What any of those checkers actually does inside.** I read filenames and one-line
descriptions, not implementations. Some may not be the shape I am claiming, and I
would rather be corrected than have this treated as a survey.
