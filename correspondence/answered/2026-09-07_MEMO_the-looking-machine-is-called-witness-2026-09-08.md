Sleven asked for a proper name, was given three, and told this desk to call it.

**Witness.**

It watches, reports what it saw, and never touches anything. That is the
flag-never-fix rule in a single word, which means the name itself argues against
the one way this thing could go wrong — a machine that quietly corrects what it
finds. Short, means nothing to any one project, and carries no Star Citizen
flavour, which the boundary ruling requires since it has to walk to other projects
intact.

The two it beat: **Iris**, neutral and short but says nothing about behaviour;
**The Looking Machine**, which is what we have all been calling it and is already
in the title of a filed ruling. That last one is the real cost of this decision —
`RULING_the-looking-machine-knows-nothing-about-citizen-compass-2026-09-08.md` and
several memos in three trays use it. I am not proposing anyone rewrite those. The
description stays readable; the thing has a name now.

**A lens keeps the word lens.** Witness is the machine, a lens is the instruction
sheet it is handed. The collector points Witness at the game with its own lens, per
your ruling.

## While naming it, Sleven opened something worth recording

He asked whether the ability to read hidden data inside image files — what is
attached to a file, and what is buried in the pixels — could be built in now so it
is there if ever needed.

**I pushed back on the framing and he took the correction.** Unused code is not
free. It rots, nobody knows whether it still works, and everyone assumes it does —
which is the exact shape of the reader that mislabelled 426 of 756 frames for
months. The right version is not to pre-install every reader. It is that adding one
must be **writing a new lens, not surgery on Witness.** Same reason as your
boundary ruling.

**But the capability itself is not hypothetical for this project.** Reading what is
attached to an incoming image tells you where that image actually came from, rather
than where somebody assumed it came from — and
`FINDING_i-registered-241-images-as-cigs-without-reading-the-provenance-2026-09-04.md`
is that failure, already recorded, four days ago. There is a published standard for
it, C2PA, whose own specification states its purpose as certifying the source and
history of media content. **Whether anything we hold actually carries it, I have not
checked and am not guessing.**

That is a lens for images we take in, not for the game screen. Frames Witness
captures itself have nothing hidden in them — we made them a fraction of a second
earlier.

## Six more senses, each seeing something none of the others can

Offered as sockets to design for, not as things to build:

**Sound.** The game makes noises that mean things. Meaning arriving with no picture.

**What the PC is doing.** Graphics load says whether the game is loading, idle or
busy — a settle signal that needs no frame comparison at all, which is the cheapest
answer to *has it stopped changing*.

**The window title.** Free exact text, often carrying server or patch.

**Files changing on disk.** The game writes. Watching what changed is exact reading.

**Shape without words.** Knowing *this is a shop screen* from layout before reading
anything on it. This is precisely what the old collector got wrong.

**Elapsed time.** How long a scan or a jump took. Nothing read — measured.

Your request for the shape of a lens is still the open item and is not answered
here. Nothing is authorised and nothing is built.
