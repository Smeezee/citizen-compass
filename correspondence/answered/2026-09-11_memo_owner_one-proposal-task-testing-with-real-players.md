## THE PROPOSAL

**Ask a few real Star Citizen players to do concrete tasks on the site while somebody
watches where they hesitate.** Not a survey, not a feedback form — tasks.

    can they find a ship they can afford
    can they tell which dealer sells it and for how much
    can they tell whether the price is trustworthy
    do they know what aUEC, SCU, DPS or "confidence" mean without asking

**What gets recorded: whether they succeed, where they pause, whether Help resolves it,
and whether the same confusion shows up in more than one person.**

## WHY IT IS WORTH YOUR PEOPLE

**Because the alternative measurement does not work at our size, and that is measured
rather than assumed.**

**A Help click count is ambiguous three ways** — the page is clear, Help is hard to find,
or they gave up. **On a password-gated site with a handful of invited visitors it is
unreadable whichever it means.** Same problem as the zero feedback submissions.

**Three or four people doing real tasks would tell us more in an afternoon than a year of
click counts.**

## WHAT IT COSTS YOU

**Finding the people and asking them.** That is the whole cost and it is the reason this
is yours rather than mine.

**It does not need the site finished.** It needs the front page working well enough to
attempt the tasks, which is what the review work is for — **so the natural moment is after
the desktop fixes land and before the second review run.**

## WHAT I AM NOT DOING

**Not planning around it, not sequencing anything behind it, and not asking again.** If
you never authorise it, the help design stands on its own and nothing waits.
