## WHY IT IS YOURS

## THE QUESTION, FOR EACH OF THE THREE

    erkul.games        fleetyards.net        spviewer.eu

**Does the site explain itself ON THE PAGE, and how?**

    is there a help control      where is it, what is it labelled
    does anything open itself    a tour, a first-visit tutorial, a timed prompt
    per page or one page         does the help change with the page you are on
    tooltips on terms            do they explain their own jargon in place
    where the real help lives    on the page, on a separate docs site, or nowhere

**One line per site is enough if the answer is "nothing".** A measured absence is the
result, not a failure.

## WHAT I ALREADY HAVE, SO YOU DO NOT REDO IT

**Fleetyards runs its documentation on a SEPARATE SITE** — `docs.fleetyards.net` — rather
than on its pages. **And a third party, space-foundry.com, publishes its own "FleetYards.net
Guide"**, which is the community filling a gap the site left.

**Searching for how to use Erkul returns third-party videos and an RSI Community Hub post,
not a first-party help page.**

**So my working reading — and it is what I want you to confirm or break — is that this
whole category leaves explaining to third parties.** If that holds, there is no
in-category pattern for us to copy and a help control on the page is a difference rather
than a catch-up. **If it does not hold, I have built a design on a wrong premise and I
want to know before he approves it.**

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

**Not a study of their design and not a comparison of their quality.** Only: how does a
first-time visitor to each of those three find out what they are looking at.

**And read nothing but the pages themselves.** No forum posts about the sites, no
