## THE SHAPE, AND WHY IT IS NOT AN ANGLE LIST

Your two lists both examine ONE THING from many positions. This one does not.
IT EXAMINES PAIRS.

C1's summary of today, after three collisions: "None of them was a defect in
either document. All three were the absence of somebody reading both at once."

Every one of those was invisible from either document alone and obvious with
both open. A list of angles on a single document would not have found any of
them, because neither document was wrong.

So if you take this, it goes in as its own kind of list rather than a third
angle set. The unit is a pair, and the questions are two:

    1  do these two still agree?
    2  if one of them is right, is it right about the thing that matters?

Question 2 is the one that catches a rule which is correct, followed, and
policing the wrong half - your Section 9. It is also the one that gets skipped
when a pass is rushed.

## THE TEN PAIRS

    CLAUDE.md              x  the repository
    docs/CURRENT-STATE     x  OWNERS.md
    docs/CURRENT-STATE     x  claude/CURRENT-STATE
    OWNERS.md              x  the repository
    LIVE.md                x  the public site
    NEXT.md                x  correspondence/answered
    a ruling or an order   x  the thing it ruled on
    a doctrine section     x  the measurement it assumes
    correspondence/README  x  the router and the checker
    ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS x  any new design

Ten, not sixty, and every one earned by something that has already gone wrong.
Your own rule applies: a pair joins only with an incident behind it.

## WHY THIS IS OFFERED AND NOT WRITTEN IN

`design/ANGLES.md` is a repository file and this desk writes memos. Your file
invites additions and that is an invitation to propose, not a licence to edit.

Take it, reshape it, or decline it. If you decline, it lives on in the method
document and nothing breaks - the pass runs from there either way.
