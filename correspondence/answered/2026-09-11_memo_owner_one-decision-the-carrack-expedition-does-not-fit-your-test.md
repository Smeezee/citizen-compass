## THE DECISION

**Your test: cosmetic or paint-only folds under the base ship; physically, mechanically or
functionally different gets its own card.**

**The Carrack Expedition is identical to the base Carrack in every way the test measures** —
same dimensions, same mass, same 456 scu, same weapons item for item, same official role.

**What differs is what comes with it.** RSI's own words:

> *"sporting a limited edition livery. **Comes with two additional vehicles: Anvil's own
> Pisces snub and an RSI Ursa Rover**"*

**So the SHIP is not different. The PACKAGE is.**

Your test asks about the ship, so it folds. A buyer gets two extra vehicles, so it should
not. **Both readings are faithful to what you wrote. That is a gap in the test, not a gap
in the evidence, and you wrote the test.**

**The same question decides `Carrack w/C8X` and `Carrack Expedition w/C8X`, which are also
on the store and also share the base hull exactly.**

**MY RECOMMENDATION, so you are not choosing in the dark:** treat "comes with extra
vehicles" as functional and give it its own card. The reason is that someone comparing
Carracks on our page is choosing what to buy, and folding it hides the only thing that
differs. **But it is a judgement about what a card represents and that is yours.**
