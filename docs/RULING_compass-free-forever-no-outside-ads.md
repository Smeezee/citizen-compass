# RULING BY SLEVEN — Citizen Compass is free, forever, and carries no outside ads

    from    C2, 2026-08-06, recording Sleven's ruling verbatim in substance
    for     C1 -> fold into CURRENT-STATE.md as a hard constraint
    context docs/FINDING_rsi-tos-verbatim-correction.md raised the question;
            this closes it.

---

## THE RULING

**All information on Citizen Compass is free to search on the website. No
paywalls. No tiers. No gated data. Ever.**

**No advertising or sponsorship from anyone outside the Star Citizen orbit.
Nothing unrelated to Star Citizen will ever appear on that page.**

**The only thing Sleven would entertain is CIG itself, or a sponsor CIG has
approved through their own partnerships** — and even that is an openness, not a
plan.

**If browsers or extensions start injecting ads into the page, we find a way to
strip them** — even though they are not ours and we earn nothing from them.

---

## WHY THIS IS RECORDED AS A CONSTRAINT AND NOT A PREFERENCE

**RSI ToS §XIII.D bars using RSI Fansite Content and Marks if you "charge a
subscription or access fee" OR "make arrangements to generate advertising or
sponsor revenue."**

**Free alone is not sufficient. An ad-supported free site loses the grant
exactly as a paywalled one does.** This ruling is what keeps Citizen Compass
inside §XIII.D, so it belongs in `CURRENT-STATE.md` next to the other hard rules
rather than in a session note.

**Any future session proposing ads, a sponsor, a "supporter tier", a Patreon
that gates anything, or a paid API must be refused on sight and pointed here.**

---

## ONE NUANCE C2 OWES SLEVEN — the CIG-partner carve-out is not automatic

**Sleven's intent is about topic: nothing non-Star-Citizen on the page. The
clause is about revenue, not topic.**

**A sponsorship paid by a CIG partner is still "sponsor revenue" under §XIII.D**,
however on-topic it is. The clause does not have a relevance exception.

**But Sleven's own framing already contains the fix:** *"approved CIG places."*
**CIG's explicit approval is the separate agreement the clause points at.**

**So the rule to carry is:**

> Sponsorship from CIG or a CIG partner is possible ONLY with explicit,
> written CIG sign-off for that specific arrangement. A company merely being a
> CIG partner is not sign-off. Absent that, the answer is no.

**Recorded so nobody later reads "CIG partners are fine" as blanket permission.**

---

## THE BROWSER-INJECTED-ADS POINT — correct, and stronger than required

**Ads injected by a user's browser extension are not "arrangements" Sleven made
and generate him no revenue, so they do not engage §XIII.D.** Stripping them is
not required by the terms.

**Sleven wants them gone anyway.** Recorded because it is the right instinct for
a different reason: **an injected ad on a Fan-Kit-compliant page can look like
the site's own advertising to anyone who does not know better** — including, in
the worst case, someone at CIG.

**Honest caveat: fully preventing extension-injected content is not reliably
achievable from inside a page.** Extensions run with more privilege than the
page does. **Frame this as best-effort and defensive, not a guarantee**, and do
not let a future session claim it is solved.

---

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE

**This ruling covers money. It does not touch the two live content questions,
both of which apply to a free site exactly as much as a paid one:**

                                 list. Still held. Being free never fixed this.

                                 says nothing about them. Separate permission,
                                 never requested.
