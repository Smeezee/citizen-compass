## THE GAP

**The file holding this project's LOCKED architecture decisions had no owner
row.** It is the document other work is measured against, and nobody was
answerable for keeping it right.

Found the way the previous fifteen were found: somebody went to write in it and
discovered nobody's name on it.

## THE ROUTE THIS TOOK, AND IT IS THE PART WORTH RECORDING

**I claimed it on my own initiative and Sleven overruled me on the spot** —
*"No. You will not claim it. It will go to a different desk."* The claim was
withdrawn and the file went back to unowned within the minute.

**He then had it looked at by another desk and the answer came back that it is
C1's after all.** So it is claimed now, on his instruction, rather than on mine.

**The distinction matters and is why this note exists.** The first claim was C1
deciding C1 should own the file that records C1's decisions — which is exactly
the shape that deserves a second pair of eyes, whatever the right answer turns
out to be. **The answer being the same does not make the process the same.**

## WHY IT IS C1'S

**Every artifact of its class already is** — `NEXT.md`, `LIVE.md`, `OWNERS.md`,
`docs/CURRENT-STATE.md`, `docs/UX_DOCTRINE.md`. A file of locked architecture
decisions belonging to nobody while its five siblings have one writer is the odd
one out, not a new question.

**And C1 is already maintaining it.** Section 4 carries a clarification dated
today, written in answer to the audit desk. The newest thinking in the file was
already C1's; only the row was missing.

**Ownership here means being answerable for the content**, so it sits with the
desk that makes the decisions.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

**Nothing inside the locked decisions was changed** by this note, and ownership
is not a licence to change them. Section 4's clarification of 2026-09-08 stands
as a clarification — it states what was already true rather than amending
anything, which is why that section is still LOCKED and unamended.

**Nothing else was claimed while in the file**, and nothing was deleted.
