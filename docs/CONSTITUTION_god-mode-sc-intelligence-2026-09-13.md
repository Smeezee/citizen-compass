# CONSTITUTION — Citizen Compass God-Mode SC Intelligence
**Frozen:** 2026-09-13 (design lock; prompts/persona come later)
**Status:** Living north star. Expand coverage beneath it; do not quietly rewrite the mission.

---

## 1. Purpose
Build and maintain a **perpetual Star Citizen intelligence archive** that gathers, verifies, files, and surfaces everything that matters about the verse — starting with **all Cloud Imperium Games communications**, then player-facing truth (game files, active orgs, bugs, creator field reports) — on automation that can outlive any one operator.

The product is the **filesystem + site**. Chat is the control panel, not the brain.

---

## 2. North star (CIG firehose)
Ingest and keep forever, as close to publish-time as platforms allow:

- Patch notes, patch roundups, release notes
- Comm-Links, DevTracker, Spectrum (official)
- CitizenCon and all other CIG shows/videos
- CIG YouTube, Twitch, X/Twitter, Instagram
- LinkedIn job listings (signal: what they’re hiring to build)
- Roadmap / status boards
- Historical **backfill** of everything already published + continuous **live** gathering thereafter

Agents keep gathering and updating without waiting for a human to remember.

---

## 3. Operating pillars
1. **Official archive** — CIG output is the sun; everything else orbits it.
2. **Build truth** — LIVE and PTU game-data tracks (must be actually live, not aspirational).
3. **Creator watchlist** — extract **claims/facts**, never verbatim scripts/monologues.
4. **Bugs & workarounds** — official notes + live gameplay observation.
5. **Active orgs** — day-to-day operators (pirate, shipping, etc.), not vanity shells.
6. **Ship / feature pipeline** — separate columns: **announced | teased | speculated**.
7. **Librarian/builder** — find → normalize → categorize → write into project files → index → refresh.
8. **Tool conductor** — use existing Citizen Compass tools; suggest new ones for gaps; do not reinvent or scrape Erkul/SPViewer for credit/data.

## 5. Truth & credit rules
| Class | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Game files / sealed extracts | Hard truth for that build |
| CIG posts/videos/notes | Official record |
| Watchlist creators | Field reports / analysis — paraphrase facts only |
| Reddit / random / rumor | Leads until verified |
| Erkul / SPViewer | Not ingest targets; match/beat their *questions* with our data |

Never republish creator wording as our content. Cite pointers. Multi-source overlap raises confidence; single source stays soft.
