## WHAT C2 GOT SLIGHTLY WRONG

C2 recorded: *"Sponsorship from CIG or a CIG partner is possible only with
explicit, written CIG sign-off. A company merely being a CIG partner is not
sign-off."*

**That framed CIG as an approver of something Sleven would arrange. Sleven's
actual position is that the arrangement is WITH CIG from the start.** He had
already accounted for the thing C2 raised as a gap.

---

## THE RULING, AS SLEVEN STATES IT

**Citizen Compass is free. All information on it is free to search. No paywalls,
no tiers, no gated data. Ever.**

**On advertising — the complete position:**

1. **The only advertising Sleven has any interest in is CIG, or sponsors who
   work with CIG.**
2. **Putting sponsorships on the site requires an arrangement with CIG to begin
   with.** Sleven states this as an understood precondition, not a hurdle.
3. **If advertising ever appears, it is because Sleven chose to enter that
   arrangement with CIG** — not because a third party approached him and cleared
   a bar.
4. **The advertisers would be sponsors that work with CIG.** Not
   Star-Citizen-adjacent. Not "gaming". Working with CIG.
5. **"If they don't align all the way across the board, I'm not interested."**
   Partial alignment is a no.
6. **Nothing outside Star Citizen will ever appear on the page, at any price.**
   Sleven's words: there is not enough money that could be offered.

---

## THE OPERATIVE RULE FOR ANY FUTURE SESSION

> **Advertising on Citizen Compass exists only as part of an arrangement made
> with CIG. Absent that arrangement, there is no advertising, no sponsor, no
> supporter tier, no paid placement, and no exceptions — regardless of who is
> asking or what they are offering.**
>
> **A sponsor being a CIG partner is a necessary condition, never a sufficient
> one. Alignment must hold across the board.**
>
> **This is not a preference to be re-litigated. Treat it as binding.**

---

## WHY IT ALSO KEEPS THE PROJECT COMPLIANT

**RSI ToS §XIII.D bars using RSI Fansite Content and Marks if you "make
arrangements to generate advertising or sponsor revenue... unless you enter into
a separate license agreement with us."**

**Sleven's precondition — that any advertising is arranged with CIG — is
structurally the same thing the clause points at.** The position was reached
from principle rather than from the terms, and it lands in the same place.

**Recorded so nobody reads it as a legal workaround. It is not. It is a
statement about what this site is for, and compliance is a consequence.**

---

## STATUS

**Binding. Not open for proposal.** Any future session suggesting advertising,
sponsorship, a supporter tier, a Patreon that gates anything, or a paid API is
to be refused and pointed here.

**Unchanged by this amendment:** the two content questions — CIG's written
descriptions are still not granted, and the `cstone.space` images are still a
third party's permission nobody has asked for. **Both apply to a free site
exactly as much as a paid one.**
