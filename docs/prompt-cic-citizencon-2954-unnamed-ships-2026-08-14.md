## The question, and why it is the only one worth asking

You established that **CitizenCon 2955 named every ship it showed** — Anvil Paladin,
Esperia Stinger, Grey's Shiv, all three now flight-ready. **There is no pool of
unnamed silhouettes in 2955.**

You also established the phenomenon is real: the **Anvil Auxilia** appeared as an
unidentified Atlas-platform silhouette in **2954** material and was not named until
Star Citizen Live on 2025-11-27 — thirteen months later.

**So 2954 is the one event known to have contained an unnamed ship, and nobody has
checked whether it contained others.**

where Drake Marauder, Origin M60, RSI Skylark or GATAC Hyun could legitimately live.
If the answer is no, those four names have nowhere left to hide and the question
closes properly instead of staying open forever.
