## V1 — the fleet now opens on the nose

`frame()` parked the camera at **+Z** while forward is **-Z**, so the viewer
opened on the back of all 239 hulls. One character: `d * 0.85` -> `-d * 0.85`.

**The -Z convention was verified, not taken on faith.** `place_hardpoints.py`
lines 28-34 state it, and I confirmed it independently against the actual point
clouds in `data-layer/derived/hull-geometry/`: the Sabre is **3.7 m wide at low
Z and 23.3 m at high Z**, matching the order's 369 cm / 2338 cm exactly. Nose is
a point, tail is the wings, so low Z is the nose. Aquila and Harbinger agree.

Measured through real three.js r128 across every hull: **239 of 239 now open at
negative Z**; with the sign restored, 239 of 239 open on the tail. The check has
a failure path and it fires.

## V3 — the panel no longer opens over the hull

The panel was not open by markup - it was `offerPanelOnce()`, E6's deliberate
first-visit auto-open. That call is now gone. `offerPanelOnce()`, `panelSeen()`
and `rememberPanelSeen()` are **deliberately left in place and deliberately not
called**, so reversing this is re-adding one line rather than rebuilding a
feature.

**This deleted a guarantee, and I did not delete the assertion that held it.**
`_verify_panel_findable.mjs` asserted the auto-open in section 2 AND asserted in
section 5 that somebody whose site data was cleared gets the panel opened for
them - E6's recovery path, which was its real argument, since settings persist
indefinitely and clearing storage clears the seen-flag too. Both sections are
**inverted rather than removed**, with the cost written into the file: recovery
now depends entirely on the visitor noticing the `Display` button.

`--mutate-nofirst` was the mutator that planted exactly what V3 shipped, so it
has been replaced by **`--mutate-autoopen`**, which plants the opposite defect.
A section whose mutator was deleted rather than replaced is a section that can
no longer fail.

Clean run 27 assertions green; all four mutators (`--mutate-oldlabel`,
`--mutate-autoopen`, `--mutate-nagforever`, `--mutate-session`) exit non-zero.

## One red control, and it is NOT caused by V1 or V3

`_verify_picker_deployed.mjs` boots the **built** page and dies on
`ReferenceError: NodeFilter is not defined`, thrown from `decorate()` calling
`document.createTreeWalker(root, NodeFilter.SHOW_TEXT, null)` in the glossary
path (`renderAll` -> `glossPage` -> `decorate`).

**Attribution was established by experiment, not by reading.** I reconstructed
the exact pre-V3 source, rebuilt from it, and booted the resulting page: it
crashes identically. So the defect is in the build output and predates this
work. `NodeFilter` appears nowhere in `checks/_loadout_harness.mjs` - the
harness simply does not provide that browser global, and the source page never
reaches the call.

Source restored and rebuilt afterwards; `_deploy` is byte-identical to what is
served, so nothing was left inconsistent.

Not fixing it: it belongs to the glossary workstream and is outside this order.
Flagging it because it is red now and somebody should own it. It is also a
textbook case for the real-browser decision - a browser global the stub harness
lacks is exactly what F3 exists to stop mattering.
