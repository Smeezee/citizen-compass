## Finding 2 — and neither did 4.10, which contradicts C4's premise

C4 exists because *"this must be re-run on every patch, because default bindings
change."* **Between 4.9 and 4.10 they did not.**

- 50 actionmaps in both. Same names, none added, none removed.
- 1,103 actions in both. Same names, none added, none removed.
- **0 of 1,103 action definitions differ** - compared on every attribute of the
  action and of its child elements, not just on names.

The two files differ by 383 bytes, and all 383 are on **one line**: 4.10 writes
`/&gt;` where 4.9 wrote `/>`, followed by ~380 trailing spaces, on the
`actionmap name="player"` line. That is a decoder-output artifact, not a game
change.

So the keybind pages were **not stale in substance**. They are now provenance-
correct against the installed build, which is the part that was actually
missing.
