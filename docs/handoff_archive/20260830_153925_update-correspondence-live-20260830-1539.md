My first draft of the memo to the Owner gave him `Copy-Item -Force` and told him
the old binary was already safe. **Neither was true** — the move-aside is the
step the permission layer blocked, so nothing had been preserved, and `-Force`
would have overwritten the only copy of a binary currently doing its job. The
memo now moves it to `_to_delete/` first and says plainly that the earlier
version was wrong.
