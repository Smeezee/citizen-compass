picks the nearest thing**, and the output is indistinguishable from a correct

because nothing downstream can tell it happened. **It carved an exception into
hard rule 11 and stood for three revisions.**

discarded. **The mechanism now has no way around itself.**

says nothing was read. **An honest unknown tells you what to teach next. A guess
never reports one.** Q44 and Q45 are the same rule arriving from two directions,
which is what C1 said and it holds up.
