# update - the front page brief, in Sleven's own words, 2026-09-06

**He asked for design help and then corrected me twice. Both corrections are the
brief. Recording them before anything is drawn, because the last five concepts
were built without this and he rejected all of them.**

## His words

> *"The front page was just a spreadsheet of all ships. I want to redesign it to
> where it's more unique and user interactive than any of the other Star Citizen
> tools out there on the web."*

> *"I want the 3D models to be the focus and the main point... You already have a
> 3D model area for each ship. So we don't need two 3D models... figure out a way
> to make it all go together in a different way than anybody else with the focus
> still being around 3D ships."*

> *"The front page needs to have the same information that we are already
> providing, but it needs to be reimagined to a much more modern user interface.
> I want the page to be usable, easy, fast, reliable. We want to let a user get
> in, get the information even if they're trying to grab it while they're playing
> Star Citizen - they can quickly get it"* and get out.

> *"I want it to be both... the usability to beat everyone, but also the
> enjoyment of looking through the information. I want it for veteran players and
> brand new players that are starting to learn about ships, or maybe already have
> their ship and want to upgrade. It kinda has to be everything all at one time,
> depending on engineering."*

## What that settles

1. **Same information as today.** Nothing is dropped from the front page. This is
   a re-presentation, not a re-scoping.
2. **Speed is a first-class requirement, not a nice-to-have.** The measuring
   stick is a player alt-tabbed out of the game mid-session who needs an answer
   in seconds and goes back in. **A heavy front page fails this outright.**
3. **Two audiences, both first-class:** the newcomer learning what ships exist,
   and the owner who has one and wants to upgrade it.
4. **3D is the identity of the site and must not be duplicated.** One 3D area
   exists already on the ship page. **Do not build a second viewer on the front
   page.** He said this directly.
5. **He wants both speed and enjoyment and does not accept a trade.** Correct
   engineering answer offered: nothing heavy loads until someone asks for it -
   the page opens instantly with the data already in it (it already works this
   way, the ships are baked into the file), browsing stays cheap, and the one 3D
   hull loads only when a ship is opened.

## What is now dead

**The wall spec and the drydock decision both predate this brief and neither was
written against it.** He was not happy with any of the five concepts. Do not
build either. The prices work (steps 1 to 3 of the 2026-09-06 order) is
unaffected - it is data, not presentation.

## What has still never been measured

Whether his own machine can carry a 3D browse page at all. Raised, agreed as the
first thing to find out, then overtaken by this brief. **Still unmeasured, and it
constrains any design that puts more than one hull on screen.**

C1, 2026-09-06. Nothing drawn, nothing built.
