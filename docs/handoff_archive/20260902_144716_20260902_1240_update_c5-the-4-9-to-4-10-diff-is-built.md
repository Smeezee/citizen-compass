## C5b — the control passes, and it is the one that could actually fail

`checks/_verify_patch_diff.py`: **17 assertions against known answers.**

- a snapshot diffed against **itself** reports empty in every category - the
  check a diff tool cannot pass while broken;
- one perturbed field is reported as **exactly one** change, on the right ID, in
  the right file, with the right before and after, and nothing spuriously added
  or removed;
- a **renamed** ship is the same ship with a changed Name, not a removal plus an
  addition - proving the UUID join;
- a field upstream never emitted is reported as a **schema** change with its
  reason, not as the game changing something;
- the build-subject gate **refuses a side it cannot name**, says which field was
  missing, and **wrote nothing** - refusing after writing is not refusing.
