**Q58 — DONE-WHEN** no note on the new front page loses words that change what it

**The defect, as recorded:** `.note` is `-webkit-line-clamp:2` in a 152px
fixed-height card with `overflow:hidden`. 88 of 253 ships carry a note and 14 run
past 100 characters and truncate. **The worst one loses the words that mark the

the card presents a contested number as settled.**

**That is the part that makes this not cosmetic** — a clamp defeating the
project's own rule about admitting what we do not know.

**The constraint, and I am holding it:** *"The card's fixed height is a ruling
and stands. Solve it inside that."* So no taller cards.

**How I intend to start, and why it is a measurement and not an edit:**

1. **Re-measure rather than trust the entry's figures.** They were taken on
   2026-09-08 and the data has moved since — the RAPTOR's note went empty today
   under Q57, so at least one number in that entry is already stale.
2. **Measure what is actually CLIPPED, in a browser, per card** — `scrollHeight`
   against `clientHeight` on the real `.note` element at real widths. **The
   entry counts notes over 100 characters, which is a proxy. A character count
   is not what a clamp does** — a short note at phone width can clip and a long
   one at desktop may not.
3. **Then decide, and the decision comes after the numbers**, because "solve it
   inside a fixed height" has more than one honest answer and which is right
   depends on how many cards clip and by how much.

**What I will not do:** make the cards taller, or shorten the notes by editing
the data to fit a box. **A note trimmed to fit is the same defect committed on
purpose.**
