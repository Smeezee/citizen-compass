rework or a Gatac Hyun was mentioned at all, a broadcast is where.

Pull captions/transcripts and search them. YouTube auto-captions are usually
available via the transcript panel under the video.

**Channels:** the official *Star Citizen* YouTube channel above all.

    Star Citizen Live          ship-team Q&A episodes are the highest-value

### 2D. Reddit — signal only, never evidence

r/starcitizen and r/starcitizen_refuge. **Use it to find the trail, not as the
answer.** What is wanted from Reddit is a *pointer*: "this was said in ISC episode
X" or "here is the dev reply". Then go and read that primary source and cite it.

**A Reddit thread is never a source for a fact about CIG's plans.** Plenty of
them are people repeating the same leak back to each other, which reads as
corroboration and is not.

## 3. Evidence rules — these decide whether the work is usable

**Counts as official:** CIG's own site, a comm-link, a CIG developer's tagged
Spectrum reply, or something a named CIG employee says on an official broadcast.

**Does not count, no matter where it sits:** player posts on Spectrum — including
on robertsspaceindustries.com, which is a trap. The previous pass hit a fan-made
*"[Concept] Drake 'Marauder' Gunship"* post sitting on CIG's own domain that looks
official at a glance. Reddit. Fan news sites. YouTube commentary channels
discussing a leak.

**No leak aggregators.** Not as a source, not as a lead, not as "let me just check
what they said". If a page is republishing a leak, close it. This whole job exists
because a leak list turned out to be roughly half wrong.

**Say which tier every fact came from.** A page that repeats another page is not a
second source.
