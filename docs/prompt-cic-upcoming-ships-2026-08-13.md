## 1. Read this before starting, because it decides the whole job

**Two different things are in that screenshot and they have completely different
evidence weight.**

**The ships are real.** Spot-checked one already: the Anvil Auxilia is an
officially announced Anvil ground vehicle on the Atlas platform, revealed
2025-11-27 on *Star Citizen Live: Lots of Ship Talk*. That is verifiable and
citable.

**The dates are not.** "Final pass deadline" is internal production language.
Nothing in that screenshot is a CIG publication. The chain is a leak aggregator,
into a video, into a phone photo — and the video's own caption already hedges it
with *"this doesn't mean the ship will release at that day exactly."*

**So the job is: verify the ships. Not the dates.** If an official source happens
to state a date, record that instead and cite it. Do not go looking for
confirmation of the leak — that is chasing a rumour to make it feel solid, which
is how a rumour ends up on the site looking like a fact.

## 3. What to collect, per ship

For each of: **Anvil Auxilia, Greycat MFC, Origin M60, GATAC Hyun, RSI Skylark,
Drake Marauder, Anvil Liberator, Kraken**, and the **RSI Constellation series
rework**:

    manufacturer, full name, and any variant names
    role / focus as CIG states it
    length, width, height, mass
    crew, cargo capacity
    production status in CIG's own vocabulary
      (concept / in development / in production / flight ready)
    where and when it was ANNOUNCED — the show, the post, the date
    the official store or Ship Matrix URL
    whether it has ever been purchasable, and how (pledge / in-game / event)

**Preferred sources in order:** robertsspaceindustries.com (Ship Matrix, Comm-Links,
Roadmap), then starcitizen.tools, then anything else. **Say which source each
fact came from, per fact.** A page that merely repeats the same leak is not a
second source, and CIC has been caught by that shape before — the `.glb` generator
tag on 2026-08-07 was true upstream and false on our copies.

## 4. The one item with a real consequence for the site

**"Constellations getting a Mk5. Mk4 (current models) getting gold standard."**

If that is true in any form, **our four Constellation models become models of the
old airframe.** We already know the Aquila is missing from the library entirely.
A rework would mean the geometry, the hardpoint derivation built on it, and the
ship pages all describe a ship that no longer looks like that.

**This is the one to dig hardest on**, and it is answerable from official sources
— a gold-standard pass is normally roadmap or Comm-Link material, not a secret.
Find out whether CIG has said anything publicly about a Constellation rework, and
if so, what and when. If they have said nothing, say so plainly; that is a useful
answer.

Community threads discussing a "Constellation Mk 5 rework" and "Constellation
series Gold Standard" exist on Spectrum. **Player speculation on an official forum
is still player speculation** — treat it as evidence that the topic is live, not
that the plan is real.

## 5. How the dates get recorded, if at all

Under the preservation model's weakest tier — **testimony**, and arguably below
it, since nobody in the chain is named beyond an aggregator handle.

If they are stored at all, they are stored as *"a leak aggregator claimed X on
this date, seen in this video"* — never as a schedule, never in a field that a
front end would render as a fact, and never without the source and the date the
claim was made. **A date with no owner is exactly the shape of thing that gets
quoted back as fact six months later.**

## 6. Not for CIC to decide, and flagged rather than actioned

**Whether Citizen Compass should host leaked CIG production schedules at all is
Sleven's call and nobody else's** (rule 8). The site operates under CIG's Fan Kit
Agreement and has an approval on record from 2026-07-28. Publishing internal
deadlines is a different act from publishing ship specs, and it touches a
relationship rather than a data model.

CIC should collect the **official ship information** either way — that is useful
whatever Sleven decides about the leak itself. **Do not publish, stage, or write
the leaked dates anywhere public as part of this job.**
