# DECISION — a shared 3D model is CORRECT unless the two ships differ in external shape. Sleven's ruling, 2026-08-14. This closes the "24 duplicate models" question and should stop it being rediscovered as a bug.

    decided by  Sleven, 2026-08-14
    recorded by C3 (Cowork)
    binding on  the holo viewer, the model library, and any future audit of it
    dataset     data-layer/derived/model-fingerprints/model_integrity.json rev 2

---

## The ruling, in Sleven's terms

The Kraken and the Kraken Privateer look near-identical in CIG's own concept art —
**the differences are internal.** So a Privateer model that looks like a Kraken is
fine. The two ship pages carry what actually differs, and that comes from CIG's own
data: components, DPS, loadout, crew, cargo. Those produce different numbers on
different pages, and that is where the distinction belongs.

Same for the rest of the shared models. **Unless the ship is physically different in
shape from its variant, it does not matter.**

## Why this is right, stated as the engineering rule

**The viewer renders bare geometry** — wireframe / hologram, no textures, no
materials, no interiors. That is not a limitation to work around; it is what the
thing is.

It follows that:

    a livery difference   is invisible in it BY CONSTRUCTION
    an interior difference is invisible in it BY CONSTRUCTION
    an external shape difference is the ONLY difference it can express

So the only defect a shared model can have is **an external shape difference that
the geometry fails to show.** Everything else is not a defect, and flagging it would
be telling the reader about a difference the picture was never going to contain.

**This also means the earlier framing was solving the wrong problem.** Rev 1 of the
integrity file would have flagged twelve paint variants for sharing a hull. That is
noise generated by testing the model against the ship's identity instead of against
what the viewer can render.

## What still gets flagged, and why it survives the rule

**`Carrack_w_C8X.glb` and `Carrack_Expedition_w_C8X.glb`.** A docked C8X snub is an
external shape difference — precisely the kind the viewer exists to show. Both files
are byte-identical to `Carrack.glb`, so they do not contain it. **This is not a
livery or interior question, which is why the ruling does not dissolve it.**

**`Aurora_LX.glb`.** Not a shared-hull case at all — the reverse. Four Aurora Mk I
variants share one 267,000-vertex model and match CIG's published 4.5 m height. The
LX has its own 28,153-vertex model measuring 3.733 in height — 14% short, against a
figure CIG publishes identically for every Mk I variant including the LX. **The file
that does not share is the one that disagrees with CIG.**

Two flags, out of 47 files. Everything else is recorded as correct.

## The test, and its honest weakness

Shape agreement is tested against **CIG's published length, width, height and mass**.
Every shared-hull pair matches CIG on all four figures.

**This is a proxy and a weak one.** Two ships can share a bounding envelope and
still differ in shape — different wing profile, same overall extents. The dimension
test cannot see that. It is what is available, and **anybody who stands next to both
ships in game beats it.** If someone reports a visible difference, that outranks
this file and the flag gets added.

## Why this is written down at all

Because a fresh pass over the library will find 24 files that are copies of another
model and will file them as a data-quality bug — **that is exactly what happened
today.** Without this decision on record, the next session redoes the work and
reaches the wrong conclusion again.

This is the same instinct as `last_verified_patch` and as the claim register:
**record the reasoning, not just the result**, so a later reader knows the shared
models were examined and kept, rather than never noticed.

## Revisit conditions

The ruling is conditional on the viewer staying texture-free and exterior-only. It
should be reopened if any of these change:

- the viewer gains **textures or liveries** — paint variants would then be visibly
  wrong on a shared hull
- the viewer gains **interiors** — the C8/C8R and Cutlass Black/Steel cases would
  become real
- a **better model source** appears that distinguishes ships this library does not
- somebody reports from in game that two ships sharing a hull **plainly do not look
  alike**

Until one of those happens, shared hulls are correct and are not to be re-flagged.
