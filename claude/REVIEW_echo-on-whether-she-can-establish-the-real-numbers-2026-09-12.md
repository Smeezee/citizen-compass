# WHAT SHE GOT RIGHT, AND TWO OF THEM ARE INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION

**THE SENTINEL. She diagnosed it herself.** *"`3.402823e+29 Gm` is definitely not a
legitimate range. It is a floating-point sentinel representing an unlimited or undefined
boundary that the page mistakenly treats as a normal value."*

**That is what the repository audit found this morning and what this desk ruled on hours
ago** — float32 maximum with the metres-to-gigametres divide already applied, on all 59
quantum drives in `LOADOUT_PARTS`, imported as a measurement. **Two instruments, two
routes, same answer.**

**AND SHE REACHED THE RULING TOO:** *"the honest result is UNKNOWN, not a guessed range."*
**This desk ruled the repaired value is ABSENT, not large, and for the same reason.**
Nobody told her.

**THE CORRECTION TO HIS PREMISE IS THE BEST THING IN THE ANSWER.** *"There often is no
timeless actual number."* Patch, component, fire mode, ammunition, capacitor allocation,
cooling, pilot versus turret. **She refused the question as asked and gave a better one,
which is the behaviour this project rewards.**

**It is also our own rule in her words.** Name the surface in every number; a figure
without its population is not a figure. **And her four required fields — patch, source,
method, confidence — are `last_verified_patch` plus the confidence field this project
already carries.** She is converging on our schema without having seen it.

**AND SHE SPLIT HER OWN CAPABILITY HONESTLY:** 9/10 to build a sourced reference, 6/10 to
prove it matches live gameplay. **Most would have given one number.**

## 2. HER SOURCE HIERARCHY PUTS EXTRACTED GAME FILES FIRST, AND SHE FLAGGED THE PROBLEM WITH THAT HERSELF TODAY

**Her own research this morning recorded that Fan Kit permission does not automatically
cover material extracted from the game.** Her source hierarchy now opens with *"extracted
files from the current game build."*

**Neither she nor anyone else connected those two, and they were written hours apart.**

**Two things are true and both matter.** The project already consumes extraction-derived
data — the scunpacked snapshot — so this is not a new exposure. **And the rights position
on it is open, recorded, and Sleven's alone.**

**So it is not a stop. It is a thing she must not decide, and her hierarchy currently
decides it.**

## 3. THE REFERENCE SHIP IS THE WRONG ONE, AND IT IS AN EASY FIX

**Her recommendation — validate one ship completely and use it as the reference before
scaling — is right and is this project's own discipline.**

**But she picked the Sabre Raven, and that is one of the thin rows.** It is an edition, it
is among the 24 with no store link, and it sits in exactly the group we already know is
under-covered.

**A reference case must test the METHOD, not the data gaps.** Pick a ship with complete
coverage — the Cutlass Black, or the Vulture — and the Raven becomes the second case,
where a gap means something.

**And the sentinel does not argue for the Raven:** it is on all 59 drives, so every ship
shows it.
