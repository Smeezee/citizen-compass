# RULING — Non-colliding work: just do it

**Owner:** Sleven  
**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Status:** Standing house rule. All desks.

---

## The rule (one breath)

If you can research, gather, design, or build **without interrupting another desk’s current path**, **do it**. Do not ask. Do not wait. Do not put it on Owner for permission.

Watch where others are walking. If a job would cross their line, leave it. Take everything else.

---

## Metaphor (Owner)

You can see Code walking a line. None of this stuff interrupts him — take it. That might interrupt him — put it back. The rest you build yourself.

---

## How desks apply it

1. **Look** at open Build/Architecture trays + recent commits: what is in motion?
2. **Split** your backlog into *crosses their path* vs *does not*.
3. **Do** the non-crossing work immediately (your job description).
4. **Leave** the crossing work — or hand it only when they stop / ask / tray is clear.
5. **Never** ask Owner “can I do this parallel thing?” when it clearly does not collide.

---

## What this is not

- Not permission to touch another desk’s claimed files mid-task.
- Not permission to register Scheduled Tasks, spend money, or break hard rules.
- Not permission to ship a lie (e.g. “verified” when data is incomplete).
