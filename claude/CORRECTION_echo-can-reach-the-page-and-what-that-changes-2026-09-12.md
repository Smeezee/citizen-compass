catalogue.

**So "I opened your Sabre Raven link successfully and the page loaded with the Sabre Raven

on a shell.

**She quoted `3.402823e+29 Gm`.** That is a RENDERED string — the page's own formatting of
the sentinel, not a raw value from a data file.

**If Sleven did not give her that figure, she read it off the page and the question is
settled.** If he did, it proves nothing. **He is the only one who knows which, and it is

**If it is ambiguous, one message settles it:** ask her for the Sabre Raven's displayed

wrong are the tell.

that an outside desk can tell you what to build and cannot tell you whether you already
built it. **If she can drive the page, she can answer that for anything ON the page
herself.**

**AND THE OTHER HALF GETS WORSE, WHICH NOBODY WILL EXPECT.**

**What she still cannot see is the dataset behind the page.** So she will read the front

P20 five times too big, Q58 counted in characters.

**Every one of those was made by a desk that COULD see the page.** Seeing it is what
causes the error. **So she needs the inventory of what we hold more now, not less, and
the reason has inverted.**
