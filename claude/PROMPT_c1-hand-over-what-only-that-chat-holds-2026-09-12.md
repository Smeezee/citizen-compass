# PROMPT — ask the old C1 chat for what only it holds

**Filed 2026-09-12 by the Adjutant desk. For Sleven to paste into the outgoing C1 session
before it is wiped and the merged desk boots.**

---

===== PROMPT START =====

Stop work. Do not file anything new, do not answer your tray, do not start a job. This session is being closed and replaced, and this is the last thing I need from it.

Everything you know that reached disk survives. Everything that lives only in this conversation dies with it. So hand that over.

WHAT I WANT

1. Anything you decided, worked out or concluded in this chat that never made it into a document, a memo or a queue entry.
2. Reasoning behind entries you filed — where the entry records WHAT and this chat holds WHY. Especially where the why would stop somebody undoing it.
3. Anything you were mid-way through. What you were doing, how far you got, and what the next step was going to be.
4. Anything you are waiting on, from me or from another desk, that is not written down as a blocker in a letter.
5. Things you suspect but have not proven, and why you did not file them. A suspicion with a reason is worth more than a clean record.
6. Where you think the record is wrong, stale or contradicts itself.
7. What you would tell your replacement in its first five minutes — the traps, the things that look settled and are not, the mistakes this desk keeps making.
8. Anything I said to you in this chat that changed a decision, where my exact wording matters. Quote me rather than paraphrasing.

WHAT TO LEAVE OUT

- Nothing about the Looking Project, the Looking Machine, the Lens or the Witness. Separate project, does not carry over.
- Nothing already on disk. If you are not sure whether something was filed, include it and mark it "not sure this was filed" — a duplicate costs me a minute, a loss costs me the thing.
- No passwords and no credentials.

HOW TO GIVE IT TO ME

One document, plain text, in this order:

    UNFILED         decided or concluded here and never written down
    THE WHY         reasoning behind filed entries that the entries do not carry
    MID-FLIGHT      what you were in the middle of, and the next step
    WAITING ON      blockers that are not written in a letter
    SUSPECTED       not proven, with the reason you held it back
    RECORD WRONG    where the written record is stale, wrong or self-contradicting
    FOR THE NEXT DESK   the five-minute briefing
    MY WORDS        anything of mine worth quoting exactly

One line under each item saying where it came from — my words, your conclusion, or something a desk reported to you here.

If this chat genuinely holds nothing the record does not already have, say that plainly and stop. That is the right answer if it is the true one, and it is the answer a well-run desk should be able to give.

===== PROMPT END =====
