settles the premise, and it does not settle it the way either side expected.**

## THE ANSWER IN ONE LINE

**The Carrack Expedition is an edition, not a package — but every Carrack, base included,
does come with two other vehicles.** Sleven was right that Carracks arrive with other ships
attached. He was wrong that this is what makes the Expedition an Expedition. Both halves
matter, and only one of them was being designed against.

**Under our own fold rule — cosmetic or paint-only folds into the base ship — the Carrack

The reasoning he gave for it does not.

## THE RENDER IS NOT EVIDENCE, AND HE PROVED IT

His own observation, and it is the sharper half of the read: the Carrack Expedition art
shows extended arm structures on the top; the Carrack Expedition w/C8X art does not; the
Carrack w/C8X art does.

**Those three SKUs differ only in livery and in which snub is included.** No structure
appears or disappears between them. **RSI reuses marketing renders across SKUs
inconsistently, so a picture is never evidence of a variant difference.** The variant matrix
is; the art is decoration.

**Adopted as a method rule.** It applies directly to our own images: 247 ship pictures, and
none of them tells you what a ship is.

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE OF THE CARRACK FAMILY

Four rows in our catalogue, and they are one ship with two independent binary options:

    livery  standard  |  limited edition ("Expedition")
    snub    C8 Pisces |  C8X Pisces Expedition

    Carrack                     standard livery, C8
    Carrack Expedition          edition livery,  C8
    Carrack w/C8X               standard livery, C8X
    Carrack Expedition w/C8X    edition livery,  C8X

**Every one of the four also comes with an RSI Ursa Rover.** RSI states the containment in
the bullet list, not the description — which is exactly where CIC's first sweep missed it.

### CONTESTED — SLEVEN DISPUTES THAT THE BASE CARRACK CARRIES A SNUB AT ALL

**His objection, 2026-09-12:** if the base Carrack already came with a Pisces, RSI would
not sell a separate "w/C8X" SKU. The existence of the second SKU implies the first has
nothing.

**What the evidence says.** CIC quotes the base Carrack's own bullet list verbatim:
*"Comes with two additional vehicles: Anvil's own Pisces snub and an RSI Ursa Rover"*, and
the w/C8X row as *"a souped-up Pisces C8X snub and an RSI Ursa Rover"*. Read that way the
two SKUs differ in WHICH Pisces, not in whether there is one — which explains the naming
without contradicting the quote.

**Neither side is proven.** The quote is evidence; his reading is inference; and CIC's
sweep of this exact question was wrong once already. **One look at the base Carrack page's
own bullet list settles it, and it has not been done.** Until it is, the ten-ship "comes
with" list is provisional in its membership, not in its shape — the relationship exists
either way, and only which rows carry it is in question.

## THE THIRD RELATIONSHIP NOBODY HAD NAMED

The design so far has two shapes, edition and package. **The Carrack is neither: it is a
ship that COMES WITH other ships, and there are ten of them.** CIC's verbatim list: both
Carracks and both w/C8X rows, Constellation Andromeda, Aquila, Phoenix and Phoenix Emerald,
600i Explorer, 890 Jump.

**All eight contained vehicles are already rows we carry** — C8 Pisces, C8X Pisces
Expedition, Ursa, P-52 Merlin, Lynx, P-72 Archimedes, G12, 85X. Eight of eight. **So this
relationship costs nothing to build: it is a link between two rows that both already
exist**, and it is the single most useful thing on a Carrack page for the buyer this site
is for.

**It is not the same as a package and must never be merged with it.** A package is several
products sold as one product. "Comes with" is one product that arrives with vehicles in it.

## PACKAGES DO EXIST, AND WE DO NOT CARRY ANY

RSI keeps them in a separate catalogue at a separate path, 14 products when CIC read it —
the starter packs, Ultimate Explorer, Industrial, Escort, Convoy, Multicrew, Dominus,
Praetorian. **None of those 14 names is among our 253 rows.** CIC's sweep was correct and
its headline was too narrow: none of our rows is a package because packages are not in the
catalogue we swept.

**The separate fact that matters more commercially: 53 of our rows carry RSI's own sentence
"This vehicle is only available as part of a PACKAGE."** The Carrack and the Carrack
Expedition are two of them. That is availability, it moves, and it is not a property of the
ship. It does not go in the same structure as anything above.

## WHAT CHANGES

**The "Packages containing this ship" section has no data behind it and is not built.** We
carry no package rows. Building the section would mean sweeping RSI's package catalogue —
a separate job with its own reconciliation, which CIC explicitly did not do.

**What gets built instead is "Comes with", on ten ships, from data we already hold.**

**Echo's three answers survive intact.** Nothing she ruled on turns out to have rested on
the wrong premise: the fold rule, the store-link-as-evidence rule and the paint position all
hold. What changes is which relationship the Carrack is an example of.

**The fold test was applied correctly and for the wrong reason, twice.** The outgoing C1
withdrew a Carrack recommendation over exactly this and wrote the reason down: the test was
applied because it was Sleven's test, without asking whether it fitted the thing. It fitted.
Nobody had checked.

## THE RULE THIS EARNS

**A ship's relationship to other ships is read from the bullet list, not the description,
not the name, and never from the picture.** All three of the wrong sources were tried this
week and all three gave a wrong answer.
