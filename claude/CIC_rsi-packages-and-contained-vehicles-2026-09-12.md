**Read 2026-09-12 from RSI's own pledge store. Nothing from a wiki, tracker or
aggregator. Nothing under `/media/`. No contact with CIG of any kind.**

Method: every one of the 253 Pass 1 URLs opened and read individually — the ship's own

## 1. HOW MANY OF OUR 253 ROWS ARE PACKAGES? **NONE.**

**Every one of the 253 rows is a vehicle product in RSI's ships catalogue.** Not one of
them is a package product.

## 4. WHAT IS NOT EVIDENCE, RECORDED SO NOBODY LATER READS IT AS EVIDENCE

**Capacity language is not containment, and several ships have it.** The Idris-M is
"capable of accommodating two medium fighters and a small ground vehicle"; the Galaxy's
"small ships and ground vehicles can be housed in the rear onboard hangar"; the Tyilui has
"four pads … for snub ship transportation"; the Liberator "offers extreme-range deployment
for light and medium fighters". **None of them states that anything comes with the ship.**
They describe what will fit. **These are deliberately excluded and are the single most
likely thing to be mistaken for a package.**

**Component language is not containment either** — "Includes a Size 1 shield" (C8X Pisces
Expedition), "comes with a pair of Behring-quality lasers" (Aurora Mk I MR), "includes an
optional missile pod" (Starfarer Gemini), "includes sequential serial numbering"
(Nautilus Solstice Edition). Loadout, not vehicles.

**A name is not evidence.** `Carrack w/C8X` and `Carrack Expedition w/C8X` are in section 2
because RSI's bullet list says so, not because of what they are called.

## 5. ONE METHOD FAILURE, RECORDED BECAUSE IT NEARLY SHIPPED

The first pass over all 253 pages used a phrase regex that **required the matched sentence
to end in a full stop.** RSI's bullet lists have no terminal punctuation, and the bullet
list is exactly where containment is declared.

**That pass reported Constellation Andromeda, Constellation Aquila, 600i Explorer,
890 Jump, Carrack w/C8X and Carrack Expedition w/C8X as carrying no containment language
at all.** All six do. The pass was discarded and all 253 re-read.

It was caught by spot-checking a page whose answer was known from outside the sweep rather
than by anything in the sweep itself. **A clean reconciliation says every row was read. It
says nothing about whether the test applied to each row was the right test.**
