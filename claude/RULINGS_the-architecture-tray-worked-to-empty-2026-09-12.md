## THE SHAPE UNDER MOST OF THIS, AND IT HAS STOPPED BEING A COINCIDENCE

**A rule keyed to a PROXY rather than to the thing itself. Eight instances in a
fortnight:**

    the date prefix          for identity

    s.hull                   for "has a ship page"

actually does.** Question 1 passes on every instance — a header and its code do not
