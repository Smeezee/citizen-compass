# THE MEASUREMENT

**Obsidian's graph and backlinks are built from `[[wikilinks]]`. This project does not
write them.**

Counted on the 14 documents staged to this session:

    documents containing a [[wikilink]]          0
    backticked path citations like
      `claude/FINDING_....md`                    76

**Seventy-six connections in fourteen documents. Every one of them invisible to the
graph.**

**So the links are not missing. They are written in a syntax the graph does not read.**

## WHY THAT MATTERS MORE THAN IT SOUNDS

The 2026-09-10 recommendation is *"look at the graph, look at what is orphaned."*

**With our syntax, EVERYTHING is orphaned.** He opens it, sees a cloud of unconnected
dots over 848 documents, and reasonably concludes the tool does not work here — when the
truth is that the corpus is densely linked and the picture is reading the wrong field.

**That is a wrong conclusion available for ten minutes' work, and it is avoidable by
knowing this first.**

    one parse      every backticked repository path in every document

the right one and it stays.
